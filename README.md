I am [Sonjoy Kumar Paul](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sonjoy-kumar-paul-9115b6b2/), a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at [Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/), advised by [Dr. Nitesh Saxena](https://nsaxena.engr.tamu.edu/) in the [Security and Privacy in Intelligent Emerging (computing and networking) Systems (SPIES) Lab](https://spies.engr.tamu.edu/). My research focuses on **cybersecurity, cognitive security, and brainwave privacy**. I design attacks and defenses at the boundary between human cognition and system security, including speech and identity inference from consumer brain–computer interface (BCI) headsets, detection of security-warning miscomprehension from gaze and cursor dynamics, and device-linking weaknesses in end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) messengers. My work has appeared at **USENIX Security** and **APWG eCrime**, with five more papers under review.

Before my Ph.D., I spent five years as a C++ software engineer at [CodeCrafters International Ltd.](https://www.codecraftersintl.com/), most recently as Senior Software Engineer, building portfolio-management and credit-analysis software (a 2M+ line codebase) used by Fortune 500 firms. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from [Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET)](https://www.buet.ac.bd/).
